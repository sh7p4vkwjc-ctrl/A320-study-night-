Q2MEMORY ITEMS
During TCAS RA warning, if following the RA order would result in crossing the intruder altitude, what shall be the crew's action?
Always follow RA order, even if this results in crossing the intruder altitude, because these orders ensure the best altitude separation.

Q6MEMORY ITEMS

Q7MEMORY ITEMS
The aircraft is not equipped with AP/FD TCAS mode. If a CLIMB RA is triggered during approach in CONF 3 or FULL, what shall the flight crew do?
GO-AROUND: perform and follow the SRS GA mode; monitor VERTICAL SPEED by checking that it remains out of the red area of the vertical speed scale, and take over if necessary.
Q8MEMORY ITEMS

Q12ABN PROCEDURES

Q13ABN PROCEDURES
What are the objectives of the Immediate Actions in the [QRH] SMOKE/FUMES/AVNCS SMOKE procedure?
Flight crew protection; avoid any further contamination of the cockpit/cabin; communication with the cabin crew.
Q14ABN PROCEDURES
If smoke or fumes are detected by the crew, without any ECAM alert, what should the flight crew refer to?
The flight crew will refer directly to the [QRH] SMOKE/FUMES/AVNCS SMOKE procedure.
Q15ABN PROCEDURES
If the AVIONICS SMOKE ECAM alert is activated, but smoke or fumes are not perceptible, what should be the crew's consideration?
Consider a spurious alert and therefore stop the AVIONICS SMOKE procedure.
Q16ABN PROCEDURES
According to FCTM, when may the "Boxed items" in the [QRH] SMOKE/FUMES/AVNCS SMOKE procedure be applied?
They may be applied at any time, but not before the immediate actions.
Q17ABN PROCEDURES
If smoke/fumes initially come out of the cockpit's ventilation outlets, or if smoke/fumes are detected in the cabin, the crew may suspect an AIR COND SMOKE/FUMES. What can the crew expect shortly thereafter?
Several SMOKE alerts (cargo, lavatory, avionics) will be triggered.
Q18ABN PROCEDURES
When encountering volcanic ash, how many degrees should the pilot perform to exit the volcanic ash clouds?
Perform a 180-degree turn to exit quickly, because volcanic ash clouds can extend for hundreds of miles.
Q19ABN PROCEDURES
When entering volcanic ash clouds, if both engines flame out and speed indications are lost, what procedure should be used to get the required pitch attitude for the optimum relight speed?
All Engines Failure Procedure
Q20ABN PROCEDURES
For a nose-low situation with high bank angle, what is the correct order of actions?
5, 3, 6, 2, 1, 4 — Recognize and confirm the situation; takeover and disconnect AP/A/THR; recover from stall if required; adjust roll in the shortest direction to wings level; adjust thrust and drag; recover level flight.
Q21ABN PROCEDURES
Regarding the [QRH] SMOKE/FUMES/AVNCS SMOKE procedure, how shall the crew perform the "Source identification and fighting" section?
According to the source they suspect, the crew will enter ONE of the 3 paragraphs.
Q22ABN PROCEDURES
Regarding the management of smoke/fumes in-flight, if the smoke/fumes source is not immediately identified and isolated, what must the flight crew do?
Initiate the diversion before entering the identification and isolation part of the procedure.
Q23ABN PROCEDURES
For a nose-high situation, what is the correct order of actions?
6, 3, 5, 1, 2, 4 — Recognize and confirm the situation; takeover and disconnect AP/A/THR; apply nose-down pitch order; adjust thrust; adjust roll not to exceed 60 degrees; recover level flight.
Q24ABN PROCEDURES
The [QRH] SMOKE/FUMES/AVNCS SMOKE procedure contains the "Immediate Actions" section. In what case shall this section be performed?
For all cases of smoke or fumes, whatever the source.
